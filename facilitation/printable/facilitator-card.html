The script, at 0:05

Nobody says their numbers today. Not to me, not to each other. Nothing gets collected, nothing goes in a doc, and this is not an input to any review. The only thing we will say out loud is one word each. I have already told my manager they are not getting the results.

This works because it is self-report, and self-report only works when it is safe. If you shade your answers today, the only person you cost is you.

The two prompts, at 0:55

  1. Where does a polished artifact hide a thinking gap in what we ship?
  2. What did we adopt in the last two quarters that nobody has refactored since?

Reliable backup if the room is quiet: if one of our agents started producing slightly worse output tomorrow, how long until someone noticed, and how?
