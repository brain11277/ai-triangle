# Changelog

## 1.0.0, 2026-09-20

First public release of the resource kit. The framework itself was published on 2026-05-22.

### The shape of it

The framework existed in two places: a Medium article and a single hand-built page. Both
explain it. Neither lets anyone *run* it, and neither stated a license, which left readers to
assume they could do nothing and commercial users to assume nobody was watching.

This release is the usable half. Everything here is either lifted verbatim from the original
publication, or newly written to turn a description into a practice.

### What is in it

- **Specification** with normative requirements, boundary conditions and anti-uses. Names who
  the framework is *not* for, which the original did not.
- **Three practice kits**, one per vertex: method, worksheet, worked example, printable. In all
  three worked examples something goes wrong, deliberately.
- **A twelve-item assessment** decomposing each vertex into behavioural items about the last 30
  days, with a scoring map onto the interactive widget's axis, five shapes, and a printable
  plotting grid.
- **A facilitation guide** built so that nobody ever says a number out loud, plus a manager
  prebrief that prevents the request for scores rather than declining it afterwards.
- **Six failure modes.** The two from the original publication, extended, and four that only
  appear once people have been running the practices.
- **Seven diagrams** with committed PNGs, generated from the same geometry the widget uses.
- **A reference widget**: two custom elements, one file, no dependencies, no build step.
- **Two licenses**, a commercial-use page with a table of the cases near the line, and a
  trademark page whose main work is declining to overclaim.

### Corrections to the original publication

- **The Korn Ferry figure was misattributed.** The original cited "Korn Ferry Workforce 2025:
  71% of CEOs reported AI contributed to imposter feelings." That figure is from **2024**,
  concerns U.S. CEOs reporting imposter syndrome **in general**, and makes no claim about AI.
  The 2025 Korn Ferry Global Workforce Survey reports 43% of senior executives. Corrected in
  the bibliography rather than quietly dropped, and it now anchors nothing.
- **The 42% adoption-abandonment figure is now sourced.** The original cited unattributed
  "industry coverage." It is S&P Global Market Intelligence, *Voice of the Enterprise: AI &
  Machine Learning*, 2025, from a survey of over 1,000 enterprises.
- **The agentic workflows preprint now has an identifier**: arXiv:2512.08769.

### Known limits

- **The assessment is self-report**, and the research it rests on says self-report is what
  degrades. This cannot be fixed from inside the instrument. `assessment/ASSESSMENT.md` says so
  in its own section rather than leaving it to be discovered.
- **Gerlich's specific correlation coefficients are unverified.** The widely quoted r = +0.72
  and r = -0.75 could not be confirmed against the publisher's full text, and are marked
  `unverified` in the bibliography. The direction and significance of the finding are
  confirmed. A formal Correction to that article has also been published and should be read
  before citing any of its statistics.
- **Three of eight sources are preprints or vendor publications**, and two of the peer-reviewed
  ones are correlational. Stated plainly in the bibliography.
- **The PNGs are regenerated by hand**, via `tools/render-diagrams.mjs`, which needs Chrome.
  They are committed, so nobody else has to run it.
- **The trademark claim is unregistered** and the mark is descriptive, therefore weak.
  `TRADEMARK.md` says this rather than implying otherwise.
