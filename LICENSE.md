# License

Two licenses, one repository. The materials and the code are licensed differently because
they are different kinds of thing, and because a software license reads badly over a
worksheet.

Neither is an open source license. Both permit far more than people assume. Read the line
below before assuming you need to ask for anything.

## Which license covers what

| Path | License |
|---|---|
| `widget/`, `tools/` | Business Source License 1.1. See [LICENSES/BSL-1.1.md](LICENSES/BSL-1.1.md). |
| Everything else | Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International. See [LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.md](LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.md). |

"Everything else" means the framework specification, the glossary, the practice kits, the
assessment, the facilitation guide, the bibliography, the diagrams and every other document
in this repository.

GitHub's license detector reports "Other" for a repository licensed this way. That is
accurate and expected.

## Additional permission for the materials

The following is not a modification of CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It is an additional permission
granted separately by the copyright holder. You may rely on it, or ignore it and use the
plain CC license instead.

> You may use the materials without payment for your own individual practice; inside your own
> organization for its own internal use, including at a for-profit company; and for teaching
> at an accredited educational institution. The ShareAlike and attribution conditions still
> apply to anything you adapt and share.

This exists because the NonCommercial definition in CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 is genuinely ambiguous
about internal use at a for-profit company. The additional permission resolves that ambiguity
in your favour. Running a self-review block at work, handing the printable assessment to your
own team, and teaching the framework in an accredited course are all free, and you do not
need to ask.

[COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) states in plain words what does and does not
need a commercial license, including a table of the cases that sit near the line.

## Attribution

Attribution is required for anything you share or adapt. Any of these satisfies it.

Plain text:

```
The AI Triangle by Brian Rain, licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
https://brianrain.com/ai-triangle/
```

HTML, which also carries the machine-readable license signal:

```html
<p>
  <a href="https://brianrain.com/ai-triangle/">The AI Triangle</a> by Brian Rain,
  licensed under
  <a rel="license" href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/">CC BY-NC-SA 4.0</a>.
</p>
```

Slide footer, for when there is no room for more:

```
AI Triangle / Brian Rain / CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
```

If you adapt the materials, say so. "Adapted from the AI Triangle by Brian Rain" is enough,
and it protects you as much as it credits the source, because it makes clear which judgments
are yours.

## ShareAlike, in one paragraph

If you adapt these materials and share the result, the result carries CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 too.
Adapting includes translating the worksheets, recoloring the diagrams, and rebuilding the
facilitation guide as a slide deck. Using the materials as they are, quoting them, or running
them internally is not adapting, and nothing propagates.

## The name

Neither license grants any rights in the name "The AI Triangle". Both say so in their own
text. [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md) explains what is claimed, what is not claimed, and why
nominative use needs no permission.

## Two things worth knowing before you rely on this

**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 is irrevocable.** Everything published in this repository under it stays
available under it, permanently, for the versions already released. The free tier described
above cannot later be narrowed for material already published. That is a deliberate choice,
recorded here so it is not mistaken for an oversight.

**The BSL converts.** On 2029-09-20 the widget code in `widget/` and `tools/` converts
automatically to the Apache License, Version 2.0, and every restriction on it disappears for
that version. Each released version carries its own Change Date. The materials have no
Change Date; CC BY-NC-SA is permanent.
