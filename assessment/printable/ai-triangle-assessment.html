The AI Triangle: self-assessment

Quality is fixed. You are the variable.

Every item is about the last 30 days. Not your intentions, not a typical month. Yes = 2, Partly = 1, No = 0. If you are arguing with yourself about whether it counts, it is a Partly. Answer items 1, 6, 7, 9 and 10 with your calendar open.

CI of Self

  1. YPN I held a self-review block at least three times in the last 30 days.
  2. YPN I named at least one specific output I accepted that I should have challenged.
  3. YPN I produced at least one piece of work of consequence where the first draft was mine, not a model's.
  4. YPN I can name, right now and without looking it up, one thing I am worse at than six months ago.

CI of Your AI Tools

  1. YPN My highest-frequency prompts and agents live in files outside the chat window.
  2. YPN At least one has a change I made in the last 30 days that I could point to in writing.
  3. YPN I retired or merged at least one prompt or agent in the last 90 days.
  4. YPN I can name the specific failure that prompted the last change to my most-used asset.

CI of New AI

  1. YPN I held a bounded scan window at least three times, and it ended when the time ended.
  2. YPN I tested exactly one new capability against a real task, with a success criterion written first.
  3. YPN I keep a written not-now list, and at least one item has a revisit date.
  4. YPN I said not-now to something I was genuinely excited about.

Stop at the first match. The order decides the answer. There is no total: the same sum describes very different practices, so only the shape is named.
